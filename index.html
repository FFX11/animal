The Hippopotamus

The mighty "river horse" of Africa

Quick Facts About The Hippo...

  • Natural Sunscreen: They secrete a red, oily fluid that acts as a built-in moisturizer and sunscreen.
  • Speed: Despite their bulky shape, they can run up to 30 km/h (19 mph) on land over short distances.
  • Diet: They are herbivores and eat up to 35 kg (80 lbs) of grass each night.


Also They Are Big and Cute

  • Can We Pet Them?: Absolutely not! They might look cute, round, and chubby, but they are highly territorial creatures. Hippos are considered some of the most dangerous mammals in Africa. You should never approach them in the wild, even if they look calm.
